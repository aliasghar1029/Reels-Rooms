Privacy Policy — Reel Room

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Reel Room ("the App", "we", "our") is a content-scheduling tool that lets a Facebook Page owner plan Reels and photos in advance and have them published automatically to their own connected Facebook Page at a scheduled date and time. This policy explains what data the App accesses, why, and how it is handled.

1. Who This App Is For

Reel Room is used only by the Facebook Page administrator(s) who connect their own Page(s) to the App. The App does not collect data from, or act on behalf of, any third party who has not explicitly authorized it through Facebook Login / OAuth.

2. What Data We Access

  • Facebook Page information: the list of Pages you manage, your Page's name and ID, and a Page Access Token — used only to publish content (Reels, photos, and their captions) to the Page you explicitly select and connect.
  • Basic profile info (public_profile): your name and profile ID, used only to identify you as the logged-in account during the connection process. We do not store or use this for any other purpose.
  • Content you create in the App: reel/photo titles, descriptions, hashtags, thumbnails, video files, and scheduled date/time — provided directly by you.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All of your content (videos, thumbnails, captions, and schedule data) is stored in your own Google Drive account, in a folder the App creates for itself. We do not copy this content to any third-party server or database. Our backend only reads this data at the moment of scheduled publishing, in order to send it to Facebook's servers via the official Graph API.

Your Facebook Page Access Token is stored securely on our backend server solely so that scheduled posts can be published automatically without requiring you to log in again each time. It is used only to publish to the Page(s) you connected, and never shared with any third party.

4. How Your Data Is Used

  • To publish the Reels/photos you schedule to your connected Facebook Page, at the time you set.
  • To show you the current connection status of your Page(s) inside the App.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or any other third party.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose beyond operating the scheduling feature described above.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You may disconnect your Facebook Page from the App at any time from within the App's settings. Doing so immediately deletes the stored Page Access Token from our backend. Your content in Google Drive remains fully under your own control at all times — you may delete it directly from your Drive whenever you wish, independent of the App.

To request complete deletion of any data our backend holds about your account, contact us using the details below and we will remove it within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Third-Party Services

Reel Room integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Meta / Facebook Graph API
  • Google Drive API

7. Data Security

Access tokens are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections only. Access to the App's backend and stored tokens is restricted to the App owner. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

8. Children's Privacy

Reel Room is not directed at children and is not knowingly used by anyone under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
